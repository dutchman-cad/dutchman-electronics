via-tenting-spans: a four-layer board, 1.6 mm thick, with seven vias in a row.

Made with KiCad 9.0.9 (file version 20241229) by fixtures/kicad/build_boards.py. KiCad shows coordinates in millimeters with Y pointing down; the board's top-left corner is at page position (100, 100), and every number below is relative to that corner (so add 100 to x and y for the page position). The reader gives Y up: model x = 100 + X, model y = -(100 + Y).

- Edge.Cuts: one rectangle, corners (0,0) and (50,30).
- Copper layers, top to bottom: F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu, B.Cu. No stack-up table.
- Setup tenting: front and back (the board default covers both sides).
- Seven vias at y = 15, each 0.8 mm across with a 0.4 mm drill, on net SIG, at x = 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35:
  - x=5: through; no tenting of its own, so it takes the board default (top and bottom covered).
  - x=10: through; tenting front and back (both covered).
  - x=15: through; tenting none (both open).
  - x=20: through; top covered, bottom open.
  - x=25: through; top open, bottom covered.
  - x=30: blind, F.Cu to In1.Cu; no tenting of its own.
  - x=35: buried, In1.Cu to In2.Cu (KiCad writes it as "blind" too); no tenting of its own.
