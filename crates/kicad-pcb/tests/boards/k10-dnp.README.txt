k10-dnp: five resistors, each with one of KiCad's footprint flags (Do not populate and the three exclude flags), and a board variant that changes two of them, to tell not-fitted parts apart (issue #184).

Made with KiCad 10.0.8 (file version 20260206) by fixtures/kicad/build_k10_boards.py. Every number and flag below was read back with KiCad's own scripting (pcbnew LoadBoard; FOOTPRINT GetAttributes, IsDNP, GetDNPForVariant, GetFieldValueForVariant, GetExcludedFromBOMForVariant and GetExcludedFromPosFilesForVariant). KiCad shows coordinates in millimeters with Y pointing down; the board's top-left corner is at page position (100, 100), and every number below is relative to that corner (so add 100 to x and y for the page position). The reader gives Y up: model x = 100 + X, model y = -(100 + Y).

- Outline: rectangle (0,0) to (50,30) on Edge.Cuts, line width 0.1; two copper layers, 1.6 mm.
- Every part is the same as k10-one-resistor's R1:
  - footprint test:R_0603, value 10k, on the top (F.Cu), not turned;
  - two surface-mount rectangle pads 0.9 x 0.95 on F.Cu, F.Mask and F.Paste, "1" 0.8 left of the origin and "2" 0.8 right of it;
  - the 3D model "box-1608.step" (not stored in the board), offset (0,0,0), scale 1, rotation (0,0,0);
  - its Reference on F.SilkS at the origin, 1.27 high.
  Only the footprint flags differ.

The parts and their flags. KiCad writes each footprint's flags in its `(attr ...)` list, after its kind `smd`:

| part | at | flags in the file | IsDNP | KiCad's flag (FOOTPRINT attribute) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | (10,10) | `(attr smd)` | no | none |
| R2 | (20,10) | `(attr smd dnp)` | yes | Do not populate (FP_DNP) |
| R3 | (30,10) | `(attr smd exclude_from_pos_files)` | no | Exclude from position files (FP_EXCLUDE_FROM_POS_FILES) |
| R4 | (40,10) | `(attr smd exclude_from_bom)` | no | Exclude from bill of materials (FP_EXCLUDE_FROM_BOM) |
| R5 | (10,20) | `(attr smd board_only)` | no | Not in schematic (FP_BOARD_ONLY) |

KiCad 10 has no separate "exclude from board" flag on a footprint. The four flags above are all there is; board_only means the footprint has no symbol in the schematic.

The variant LITE
- The board lists it at the top level, after `setup`: `(variants (variant (name "LITE")))`. KiCad names the variants "< Default >" and "LITE"; the default has no entry of its own.
- A footprint that differs in a variant holds a `(variant ...)` entry with only what differs:
  - R1: `(variant (name "LITE") (dnp yes) (field (name "Value") (value "1k")))`. In LITE, R1 is not populated and its value is 1k.
  - R2: `(variant (name "LITE") (dnp no))`. In LITE, R2 is populated, although its own flag says dnp.
- R3, R4 and R5 have no variant entry. In LITE they keep their own flags: KiCad gives R3 excluded from position files and R4 excluded from the bill of materials in LITE too.
- The board's plot settings carry KiCad's defaults for drawing not-populated parts on the Fab layers: `(hidednponfab no)`, `(sketchdnponfab yes)`, `(crossoutdnponfab yes)`.

| part | default: populated | LITE: populated | LITE: value |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | yes | no | 1k |
| R2 | no | yes | 10k |
| R3 | yes | yes | 10k |
| R4 | yes | yes | 10k |
| R5 | yes | yes | 10k |

What the reader does with this board:
- all five parts are read and placed, with their pads and models, whatever their flags; R2 is not fitted (`dnp`), the other four are;
- the variant LITE is read: R1 not fitted, R1's value 1k;
- R2's `(dnp no)` in LITE gets a warning, "R2 is fitted in variant LITE but marked DNP on the board; the variant's fitting is not carried", since the board model cannot say that a variant fits a part the board leaves out;
- the five Value fields are on F.Fab, which is not drawn (a note, as on other boards).
