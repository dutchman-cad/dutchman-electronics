pad-arcs: custom pads whose primitives hold arcs, on a footprint that is not turned, one that is turned 30 degrees, and two on the bottom.

Made with KiCad 9.0.9 (file version 20241229) by fixtures/kicad/build_boards.py, then fixtures/kicad/add_pad_arcs.py (KiCad's scripting cannot put an arc inside a polygon, so the script writes it into the saved text and has KiCad load and save the board again). KiCad shows coordinates in millimeters with Y pointing down; the board's top-left corner is at page position (100, 100). The reader gives Y up: model x = 100 + X, model y = -(100 + Y).

Every pad is surface mount on F.Cu (bottom footprints: B.Cu). Primitives are in the pad's own frame (KiCad's Y down):
- kind "d": a circle anchor of 1.0 and a filled gr_poly that is one arc from (0,-1.5) through (1.5,0) to (0,1.5), closed by the straight edge between its ends: a half disk of radius 1.5.
- kind "arc": a square anchor of 0.6 and a gr_arc stroke of width 0.4 from (0,-1.2) through (0,1.2) to (-1.2,0): 270 degrees about the pad's center, radius 1.2, round ends.
- kind "ring": a circle anchor of 0.5 and an unfilled gr_circle of radius 1.2 and width 0.3: a ring from radius 1.05 to 1.35.
- kind "dot": a square anchor of 1.0 and a filled gr_circle of radius 0.9 centered at (0.8,0).
- U1 (top, at (6,8), not turned): d at (0,0), arc at (6,0), ring at (12,0), dot at (18,0). Pad centers in the model: (106,-108), (112,-108), (118,-108), (124,-108).
- U2 (top, at (6,22), turned 30): the same four pads. Pad 1 at (106,-122), pad 2 at (111.196152,-119), pad 3 at (116.392305,-116), pad 4 at (121.588457,-113).
- U3 (bottom, at (44,8), not turned): d at (-6,0), arc at (0,0), ring at (6,0).
- U4 (bottom, at (44,22), turned 90): d at (-6,0), arc at (0,0).
- That is 13 pads, each read as a polygon pad: the anchor plus the primitive as contours holding exact arcs (the half disk is one arc and one line; a stroked arc is an outer arc, an inner arc and two round ends; a ring is two half rings).
- How the outlines were checked: KiCad's own Gerber export of F.Cu and B.Cu (kicad-cli pcb export gerbers, KiCad 9.0.9) is committed as tests/gerber/pad-arcs-F_Cu.gbr and pad-arcs-B_Cu.gbr, with the outlines fixtures/kicad/gerber_corners.py reads from it in the matching .corners.txt files. KiCad cuts each arc into short chords (an error of about 0.0005 mm on a 1.5 mm radius). The test checks that every corner of KiCad's outline lies on our exact outline, and that every point of our outline that no other piece of the pad covers lies within 0.01 mm of KiCad's.
