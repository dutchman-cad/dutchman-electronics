model-placements: four parts, each with two pads set off-center and a 3D model reference, to show where a model lands.

Made with KiCad 9.0.9 (file version 20241229) by fixtures/kicad/build_boards.py, on the 50 x 30 mm rectangle. KiCad's Y points down; the board's top-left corner is at page position (100, 100), and the numbers below are relative to it. The model "box.step" is not stored in the board.

Every part has pads "1" (1.0 x 1.0) and "2" (1.5 x 0.8) written in the file at the position given in the library (-2, 0.5) and (1, -1.5) before turning and flipping.

| part | side | origin | angle in the file | pad 1 | pad 2 | model offset | KiCad model rotate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | bottom | (12, 15) | 90 | (11.5, 17) | (13.5, 14) | (1, 2, 0.5) | (90, 0, 0) |
| U2 | bottom | (25, 15) | -90 (pads 270) | (25.5, 13) | (23.5, 16) | (-1, 0.5, 0.25) | (0, 90, 0) |
| U3 | top | (38, 15) | 0 | (36, 15.5) | (39, 13.5) | (0, 0, 0) | (90, 90, 0) |
| U4 | top | (38, 25) | 90 | (38.5, 27) | (36.5, 24) | (1.5, -1, 0.3) | (90, 0, 45) |

The pad positions are KiCad's own (from its board scripting, GetPosition), so they show that a bottom part's pads are saved already in board orientation: the board position is the origin plus the pad's saved position turned by the angle, with no mirroring. The file holds the pads of a bottom part with their y already flipped (U1's pad 1 is saved at (-2, -0.5)).

Where KiCad puts each model: we exported each board to VRML with kicad-cli (box STEP, see fixtures/kicad/model_axes) and read each model's rotation (axis and angle) and translation. Relative to the part's origin, in board directions with Y up and z up from the middle of the board (a bottom part's z is negative):

| part | translation from the origin (x, y, z above its board face) | rotation axis | angle (rad) |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | (2, 1, -0.5) | (0.57735027, 0.57735027, 0.57735027) | 2.0943951 |
| U2 | (-0.5, 1, -0.25) | (0.57735027, -0.57735027, -0.57735027) | 4.1887902 |
| U3 | (0, 0, 0) | (-0.57735027, -0.57735027, -0.57735027) | 2.0943951 |
| U4 | (1, 1.5, 0.3) | (-0.86285621, -0.35740674, 0.35740674) | 1.7177715 |

(z: VRML gave the middle of the board as 0, so a model 0.5 below the bottom face is at -1.3, and we list it relative to the face.) tests/model_placements.rs checks that the reader's placement, offset and rotation give these.
