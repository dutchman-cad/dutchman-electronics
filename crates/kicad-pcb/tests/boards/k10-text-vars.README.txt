Original test board made for Dutchman Electronics. Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

k10-text-vars: texts that use KiCad's text variables, with the project file that holds the project's own variables. A record of what KiCad shows for each text, and of where KiCad shows the footprints' fields. The reader gives what KiCad shows for every row (crates/kicad-pcb/tests/text_vars.rs).

Made with KiCad 10.0.8 (file version 20260206) by fixtures/kicad/build_k10_boards.py and fixtures/kicad/add_text_vars.py (which writes k10-text-vars.kicad_pro). What KiCad shows was read back with fixtures/kicad/shown_texts.py (KiCad's GetShownText, the board loaded with its project); where it shows the fields, with KiCad's scripting (each field's text position, angle, size, justification and mirroring). KiCad shows coordinates in millimeters with Y pointing down; the board's top-left corner is at page position (100, 100), and every number below is relative to that corner (so add 100 to x and y for the page position). The reader gives Y up: model x = 100 + X, model y = -(100 + Y).

Where the values live:
- In the board file: the title block (title "Text Variables", rev "B2", company "Dutchman Test", date "2026-11-27", comment 1 "First comment", comment 2 "Second comment") and one board property, BOARD_NOTE = "board property".
- In k10-text-vars.kicad_pro only: the project's text variables PCB_NUMBER = "DN-0042", BOARD_ID = "${PCB_NUMBER}-${REVISION}" (a variable that uses others) and FINISH = "ENIG" (not used by any text). With the board loaded without its project, KiCad shows ${PCB_NUMBER} and ${BOARD_ID} as they are; every other text shows the same as below.

Geometry:
- Outline: rectangle (0,0) to (100,80) on Edge.Cuts, line width 0.1; thickness 1.6.
- Board texts: 1.5 mm high, pen 0.2, left-justified, vertically centered, not turned. Rows 1 to 18 on F.SilkS at x = 5, y = 5, 8, 11, ... (row n at y = 2 + 3n); row 19 on F.Cu at (5, 59); row 20 on B.SilkS, mirrored, at (45, 75).
- Footprint test:Conn J1 on the top at (70,20), not turned, value CONN-2; footprint J2 flipped to the bottom at (70,50), turned 180 by the flip, value CONN-3. Reference fields on the silkscreen at the footprint's origin (1.27 mm), Value fields on the Fab layer. Their other fields, where KiCad shows them (page coordinates less 100, so J1's origin is (70,20)):
  - MPN = "ABC-123" on J1: F.SilkS at (64,20), 1.0 mm, pen 0.15, turned 90, right-justified, vertically centered. On J2, "XYZ-9": B.SilkS at (76,50), turned 90, left-justified (the flip turns right into left), mirrored.
  - BUILD = "${PCB_NUMBER}/${REFERENCE}" (a field value holding variables): J1 on F.SilkS at (76,20), 1.2 mm, pen 0.15, not turned, left-justified, top-aligned. J2 on B.SilkS at (64,50), left-justified, top-aligned, mirrored.
  - SECRET = "hidden" on the silkscreen, hidden: J1 at (70,14), J2 at (70,44).
  - FAB_NOTE = "fab note", shown, on F.Fab at (70,17) (J1) and B.Fab at (70,47) (J2), 1.27 mm.
  - Datasheet and Description, empty and hidden, on the Fab layers.
- Each footprint has six user texts (1.5 mm, pen 0.2, left-justified) at (0, 4), (0, 7), (0, 10), (0, 13), (0, 16), (0, 19) from its origin (Y down; on J2 mirrored with the flip, so at Y = 50 + those on the page too).

Each text: the stored string and what KiCad shows. Before the reader filled in text variables (issue #156) and drew the fields (#157), it gave only the Reference fields, ${REFERENCE} and ${VALUE} in footprint texts; every other text came out as stored, and the MPN fields were missing.
