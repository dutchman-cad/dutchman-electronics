zone-thermal: a copper pour with a thermal-relief pad and a via it keeps clear of, to check the fill's holes.

Made with KiCad 9.0.9 (file version 20241229) by fixtures/kicad/build_boards.py and fixtures/kicad/fill_zones.py (KiCad fills the zone). KiCad shows coordinates in millimetres with Y pointing down; the board's top-left corner is at page position (100, 100), and every number below is relative to that corner (so add 100 to x and y for the page position). The reader gives Y up: model x = 100 + X, model y = -(100 + Y).

- Nets: GND (1), SIG (2).
- Zone on F.Cu, net GND, outline (5,5), (45,5), (45,25), (5,25); minimum width 0.25, clearance 0.5, pad connection thermal relief, thermal gap 0.5, spoke width 0.5. KiCad filled it (the fill is saved in the file as one polygon with a zero-width bridge to each hole).
- Footprint test:Pin, designator J1, at (15,15): plated round pad 1.6 mm, drill 0.8, net GND, copper on every layer.
- Via at (30,15): diameter 0.8, drill 0.4, net SIG.
- Expected copper: one region, outer contour the zone outline 40 x 20 = 800 mm2 (the corners rounded off by KiCad, under 0.01 mm2).
- Expected holes: exactly 2.
  - The via's gap: a disc of radius 0.4 + 0.5 = 0.9 centred on (30,15): area pi * 0.9^2 = 2.545 mm2.
  - The pad's gap with its thermal relief: a disc of radius 0.8 + 0.5 = 1.3 centred on (15,15) less the four spokes of copper KiCad leaves in it. Each spoke is 0.5 wide (the thermal bridge width), at 45 degrees, and runs from the drill's edge (radius 0.4) out to radius 1.3, because KiCad's fill covers the pad's own area: it carves out only the drill and the gap between the spokes. Hole area pi * 1.3^2 - 4 * 0.4599 = 3.470 mm2, where 0.4599 is the integral over t from -0.25 to 0.25 of (sqrt(1.3^2 - t^2) - sqrt(0.4^2 - t^2)) (KiCad's polygon, with its facets, gives 3.481). The test allows 0.05 mm2.
