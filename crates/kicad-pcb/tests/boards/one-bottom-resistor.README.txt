one-bottom-resistor: the same footprint on the bottom side, turned 90 degrees.

Made with KiCad 9.0.9 (file version 20241229) by fixtures/kicad/build_boards.py. KiCad shows coordinates in millimeters with Y pointing down; the board's top-left corner is at page position (100, 100), and every number below is relative to that corner (so add 100 to x and y for the page position). The reader gives Y up: model x = 100 + X, model y = -(100 + Y).

- Footprint test:R_0603, designator R1, on B.Cu at (25,15), rotation 90.
- Pads on B.Cu, B.Mask and B.Paste, rectangles 0.9 x 0.95 turned 90 degrees: "1" at (25,15.8) and "2" at (25,14.2). (In the file they sit at (-0.8,0) and (0.8,0) from the origin; KiCad's turn by 90 degrees puts the first one below the origin on screen.)
- 3D model "box-1608.step": offset (0,0,0), scale (1,1,1), rotation (0,0,0).
