# Dutchman Electronics 0.1.0

We release our first electronics module for Dutchman. We need **Dutchman 0.4.0 or later**.

## What we read

- **Altium `.PcbDoc`:** board outlines, cutouts, thickness and layer stacks; tracks, arcs, pads, vias, pours and internal planes; silkscreen text and graphics; component placements, embedded or linked STEP models, and extruded bodies, including cylinders.
- **Altium `.PrjPcb`:** the selected board and project parameters used in board text. We also find a matching project beside a board opened on its own.
- **KiCad `.kicad_pcb` (KiCad 9 and 10):** board outlines, cutouts, holes and stack-ups; tracks, arcs, vias, shaped pads and filled zones with holes; text, shown footprint fields, component placements and linked STEP models. We fill text variables from the board and its `.kicad_pro` project.

## What we build through Dutchman

We build a SolidCraft design with an exact board body and drilled holes, plated-hole linings, copper, conformal solder mask, silkscreen, laminate and surface finish. We place the file's usable 3D bodies, or standard bodies for recognized packages without a usable model. We fill project parameters in text and group components by board side.

## Known limits

- We draw outer copper by default. The module's `--copper all` option includes inner copper and Altium internal planes; individual pad and via plane-rule overrides are not read.
- We need linked models to be available locally. Mesh-only models cannot become exact solids; missing or unusable models produce notes. Standard bodies cover only recognized packages.
- We do not yet read Altium sphere bodies. Per-side pad shapes and hand-drawn mask openings have model support, but the readers do not yet fill those fields.
- We approximate Altium stroke fonts with Hershey letters. Embedded TrueType fonts use their letter outlines; unavailable fonts fall back to strokes with a note.
- We report unsupported content in notes. Large overlays may become flat sheets to stay within the face limit, also with notes.
- We still have live-link work on the roadmap; see [Dutchman Sync](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/931).

## Install

We provide Windows, macOS and Linux archives with SHA-256 checksums. Put the program in the per-user modules folder, which survives Dutchman updates; follow [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman-electronics/blob/v0.1.0/INSTALL.md) for the platform steps and checks.
