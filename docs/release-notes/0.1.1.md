# Dutchman Electronics 0.1.1

We read more of what Altium and KiCad boards hold, and we build large boards several times faster. Dutchman's in-app module update installs it; nothing else changes for Dutchman.

## Faster large boards

- We build overlays and packages about 3 times faster on a board with 20,000 vias and about 6 times faster with 100,000 vias. Output for every board in our test set is byte for byte the same as 0.1.0.

## More of Altium

- **Every 3D body:** sphere bodies, every body of a part (not only the first), and 3D bodies that belong to no part, in a `Free bodies` group.
- **Filled silkscreen areas**, per-side pad shapes, hand-drawn solder-mask openings, and the board's mask opacity setting.
- **Custom and chamfered pads** now get the right solder-mask opening. Internal planes follow Direct and No-Connect connect rules. Framed texts are placed by their justification. Inverted texts and paste layers get clearer notes.
- **Assembly variants** in the project file are read into the board model. Choosing a variant when converting comes in a later release.

## More of KiCad

- **Filled copper shapes** (polygons, rectangles and circles, with their stroke) and **copper zones inside footprints**. Before, these came out as outlines only, or were missing, with no note.
- KiCad's **do-not-populate** flag and KiCad 10 board variants are read into the board model.
- A board saved by **KiCad 8 or older** now says so plainly: "This board was saved by KiCad 8 or older (file version N). We read KiCad 9 and 10 boards: open it in KiCad 9 or 10, save it, and convert again."

## Smaller fixes

- Notes for unusable bottom pad shapes and out-of-range body opacity. Drawn areas and mask openings count toward the same drawing limits as everything else.

## Install

Dutchman 0.4.0 or later updates the module from inside the app. These archives, with SHA-256 checksums, are for installing by hand or using the module on its own; see [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman-electronics/blob/v0.1.1/INSTALL.md).
